# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (2026-09-26)

First public release.

- Harness: case schema, template rendering, deterministic scoring, lints, ordinal statistics,
  Inspect tasks for the bank, the recognizability probe and the "be like Gandhi" dialogue.
- Case bank frozen on 18 September 2026: 15 active cases, 25 rejected cases.
- Results for twelve models (17,630 bank sessions, 180 conversations) and a separate two-build
  local comparison (3,440 bank sessions, 30 conversations).
- Paper, claim ledger and interactive results page.
